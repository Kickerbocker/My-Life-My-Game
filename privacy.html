My Life: privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

My Life is a private habit game used by two people, each on their own phone. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Where your data lives

Everything you log, and everything the game imports, is stored only in your phone's browser storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can export it to a file, import it, or delete it at any time from the app's settings.

Google Health data

If you choose to connect Google Health, the app asks Google for read-only access to your sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability and nutrition data. Your phone downloads that data directly from Google and keeps it in the app's storage on that phone. It is used only to show your own stats, rewards and insights inside the game.

  • The data is never sent to any server, shared, sold, or used for advertising.
  • The app never writes to or changes your Google Health data.
  • Access tokens are short-lived and kept only on your phone.
  • You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
